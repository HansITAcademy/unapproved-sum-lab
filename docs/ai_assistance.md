# AI-assisted software development disclosure

I used OpenAI ChatGPT to assist with coding, debugging, cleanup, and implementation of this computational laboratory based on research specifications I developed.

I developed the research question, conceptual framework, experimental design, hypotheses, task logic, research methods, analysis plan, interpretation, and manuscript. I reviewed the AI-assisted code and outputs, ran the automated and deterministic validation checks, and take responsibility for the implementation and the study's findings.

The purpose of stating this plainly is not to treat generated code as a research author. It is to make the development process transparent and to separate software assistance from intellectual authorship of the research design and claims.
